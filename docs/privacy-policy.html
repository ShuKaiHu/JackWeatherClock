Rainy Clock

Privacy Policy · Last updated August 2026

Overview

Rainy Clock is an iOS app that automatically adjusts your alarm time when rain is forecast along your commute route. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is used and how.

Data We Do Not Collect

Rainy Clock does not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.
  • Have a server or backend of any kind.
  • Use analytics SDKs.
  • Track you across other companies' apps and websites without your explicit permission — see Tracking.

Data Stored On Your Device

The following settings are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults and never leave your device:

  • Home and work address text (used solely to look up your commute route)
  • Commute mode preference
  • Alarm time, rain lead time, and rain probability threshold

Third-Party Services

When you enter addresses or tap "Schedule Smart Alarm," the app contacts the following services on your behalf:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit / CLGeocoder) – to convert your address text into geographic coordinates and calculate a commute route.
  • Apple Weather / WeatherKit – to retrieve weather forecasts for your home and work areas.

Apple Maps is provided by Apple Inc. and is subject to Apple's own Privacy Policy. Apple Weather / WeatherKit is provided by Apple Inc. and is subject to Apple's own Privacy Policy. Your addresses and location coordinates are used only to calculate routes and retrieve weather forecasts.

Advertising

Rainy Clock displays one banner advertisement through Google AdMob. Google may process device, advertising, and interaction data to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads. AdMob is provided by Google LLC and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

The first time you open the app, iOS asks whether Rainy Clock may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. This is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and your answer decides what happens:

  • If you allow it, the advertising identifier on your device may be shared with Google so the banner can be matched to your interests.
  • If you decline, or if you are never asked, ads are requested as non-personalized and the advertising identifier is not used to track you.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The app works exactly the same either way — the only difference is how relevant the banner is.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a separate consent dialog appears before any ad loads, and you can revisit that choice at any time through Ad privacy options in the app.

Alarms and Notifications

On iOS 26 and later, alarms are scheduled through Apple's AlarmKit, which is what allows the alarm to sound through Silent mode and Focus. On iOS 17 through 25, alarms are scheduled as local notifications instead. Either way, alarms are processed entirely on your device and no alarm or notification content is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at shukaihu@icloud.com.

繁體中文

概覽

Rainy Clock 是一款 iOS App，會在通勤路線預報可能下雨時，協助你把鬧鐘提前。本政策說明 App 使用哪些資料以及用途。

我們不收集的資料

Rainy Clock 不會：

  • 將個人資料收集、儲存或傳送到我們的伺服器。
  • 使用任何後端伺服器。
  • 使用分析 SDK。
  • 未經你明確允許，跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤你 —— 詳見追蹤。

儲存在裝置上的資料

以下設定會使用 UserDefaults 儲存在你的裝置上：

  • 住家與公司地址文字，用於查詢通勤路線。
  • 通勤方式偏好。
  • 鬧鐘時間、雨天提前時間與降雨機率門檻。

第三方服務

當你輸入地址或點選「排程智慧鬧鐘」時，App 會代表你使用下列服務：

  • Apple Maps（MapKit / CLGeocoder）：將地址文字轉換為座標並計算通勤路線。
  • Apple Weather / WeatherKit：取得住家與公司附近的天氣預報。

Apple Maps 由 Apple Inc. 提供，並受 Apple 的 隱私權政策約束。Apple Weather / WeatherKit 由 Apple Inc. 提供，並受 Apple 的 隱私權政策約束。你的地址與座標只會用於計算路線與取得天氣預報。

廣告

Rainy Clock 會透過 Google AdMob 顯示一則橫幅廣告。Google 可能會處理裝置、廣告與互動資料，用於提供、限制、衡量與改善廣告。AdMob 由 Google LLC 提供，並受 Google 的 隱私權政策約束。

追蹤

第一次開啟 App 時，iOS 會詢問是否允許 Rainy Clock 追蹤你在其他公司 App 與網站上的活動。這是 Apple 的「App 追蹤透明度」提示，你的選擇決定接下來的行為：

  • 選擇允許：裝置上的廣告識別碼可能會提供給 Google，讓橫幅廣告更貼近你的興趣。
  • 選擇不允許，或從未被詢問：廣告會以非個人化方式請求，廣告識別碼不會用於追蹤你。

你可以隨時在「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 追蹤」更改這個選擇。無論選擇哪一種，App 的功能完全相同，差別只在廣告的關聯性。

若你位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士，在任何廣告載入前還會出現一個獨立的同意對話框，之後可隨時透過 App 內的「廣告隱私設定」重新調整。

鬧鐘與通知

在 iOS 26 以上，鬧鐘透過 Apple 的 AlarmKit 排程，這也是鬧鐘能穿透靜音與專注模式的原因；在 iOS 17 至 25 則以本機通知排程。兩種方式都完全在你的裝置上處理，鬧鐘與通知內容不會傳送到任何伺服器。

兒童隱私

本 App 不會刻意收集 13 歲以下兒童的資料。
