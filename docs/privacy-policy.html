Rainy Clock

Privacy Policy · Last updated 31 August 2026

Overview

Rainy Clock is an iOS app that automatically adjusts your alarm time when rain is forecast along your commute route. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is used and how.

Data We Do Not Collect

Rainy Clock does not:

  • Ask for your name, email address, or any account. Rainy Clock has no sign-in.
  • Use analytics SDKs.
  • Keep a database, a user profile, or request logs on any server we run.
  • Track you across other companies' apps and websites without your explicit permission — see Tracking.

Data Stored On Your Device

The following settings are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults:

  • Home and work address text (used solely to look up your commute route)
  • Commute mode preference
  • Alarm time, rain lead time, and rain probability threshold
  • How many AI voice alarms you have generated, so the free allowance can be counted
  • The words you last typed for an AI voice alarm, so the screen reopens on them

None of these leave your device, with one exception: the words you type for an AI voice alarm are sent when you generate it. See AI Voice Alarm.

AI Voice Alarm

Rainy Clock can wake you with a spoken line instead of a tone. This is the only part of the app that sends anything you have written off your device, and it only happens when you tap Generate.

  • What is sent: the words you typed, which of the six voices you picked, and whether the app is in Chinese or English. Nothing else — not your addresses, not your alarm time, not any device identifier.
  • Where it goes: to a small relay service we run, which passes it to Google's Gemini text-to-speech and returns the audio. The relay exists because the key that authenticates the request cannot safely ship inside an app.
  • What is kept: the relay holds the words and the resulting audio in memory for up to one hour, so that repeating the same request does not have to be generated again. Nothing is written to a database or to a log, and nothing is linked to you or to your device.
  • The finished alarm sound stays on your phone. It is saved in the app's own storage and is deleted when you replace it or delete the app.

Google processes the text to produce the speech, under Google's privacy policy. We use the paid tier, where Google states that the content sent is not used to train its models.

If you never use this feature, nothing you write ever leaves your phone.

Third-Party Services

When you enter addresses or tap "Schedule Smart Alarm," the app contacts the following services on your behalf:

  • iPhone — Apple Maps (MapKit / CLGeocoder) – to convert your address text into geographic coordinates and calculate a commute route.
  • Android — Google Maps Platform – the Maps SDK and Routes API draw the route and calculate its length; addresses are turned into coordinates by Android's own geocoder on the device.
  • Both — Apple Weather / WeatherKit – to retrieve weather forecasts for your home and work areas, and for points along your route.

Apple Maps and Apple Weather / WeatherKit are provided by Apple Inc. and are subject to Apple's own Privacy Policy. Google Maps Platform is provided by Google LLC and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Your addresses and location coordinates are used only to calculate routes and retrieve weather forecasts.

Weather requests on Android

Apple requires weather requests to be signed with a private key that cannot safely be placed inside an Android app, so the Android version asks Apple for the forecast through a small relay service we operate, rather than contacting Apple directly. What this means for you:

  • The relay receives only a coordinate, a time range and a language — never your address text, your device identifier, or anything that identifies you.
  • Coordinates are rounded to roughly one kilometre before being used, and the resulting forecast is held in memory for about fifteen minutes so that repeated requests for the same area do not have to be asked again.
  • Nothing is written to a database or a log of your requests, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

The iPhone version has no relay: it talks to Apple Weather directly.

Advertising

Rainy Clock displays one banner advertisement through Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). Unity and the advertising partners bidding through it may process device, advertising, and interaction data to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads. LevelPlay is subject to Unity's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

On Android there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt — the mechanism does not exist on that platform. Whether the banner is personalised is governed entirely by the consent choice described at the end of this section, which the app passes to the ad SDK. The rest of this section describes the iPhone version.

The first time you open the app, iOS asks whether Rainy Clock may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. This is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and your answer decides what happens:

  • If you allow it, the advertising identifier on your device may be shared with Unity and the advertising partners bidding through LevelPlay, so the banner can be matched to your interests.
  • If you decline, or if you are never asked, ads are requested as non-personalized and the advertising identifier is not used to track you.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The app works exactly the same either way — the only difference is how relevant the banner is.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a separate consent dialog appears before any ad loads, and you can revisit that choice at any time through Ad privacy options in the app.

Alarms and Notifications

On iOS 26 and later, alarms are scheduled through Apple's AlarmKit, which is what allows the alarm to sound through Silent mode and Focus. On iOS 17 through 25, alarms are scheduled as local notifications instead. Either way, alarms are processed entirely on your device and no alarm or notification content is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

繁體中文

概覽

Rainy Clock 是一款 iOS App，會在通勤路線預報可能下雨時，協助你把鬧鐘提前。本政策說明 App 使用哪些資料以及用途。

我們不收集的資料

Rainy Clock 不會：

  • 詢問你的姓名、電子郵件或任何帳號。Rainy Clock 沒有登入機制。
  • 使用分析 SDK。
  • 在我們營運的伺服器上保留資料庫、使用者輪廓或請求紀錄。
  • 未經你明確允許，跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤你 —— 詳見追蹤。

儲存在裝置上的資料

以下設定會使用 UserDefaults 儲存在你的裝置上：

  • 住家與公司地址文字，用於查詢通勤路線。
  • 通勤方式偏好。
  • 鬧鐘時間、雨天提前時間與降雨機率門檻。
  • 已經生成過幾次 AI 語音鬧鈴，用於計算免費次數。
  • 你上次為 AI 語音鬧鈴輸入的文字，讓畫面重新打開時能帶回原本的內容。

以上都不會離開你的裝置，只有一個例外：你為 AI 語音鬧鈴輸入的文字，會在你按下產生時送出。詳見 AI 語音鬧鈴。

AI 語音鬧鈴

Rainy Clock 可以用一段人聲取代鈴聲叫醒你。這是整個 App 裡唯一會把你寫的內容送出裝置的功能，而且只在你按下「產生」時才會發生。

  • 送出什麼：你輸入的文字、你選的六個聲音中的哪一個，以及 App 目前是中文還是英文。就這些——不包含你的地址、鬧鐘時間，或任何裝置識別碼。
  • 送到哪裡：先送到我們營運的一個小型轉發服務，再由它轉給 Google 的 Gemini 文字轉語音並取回音訊。之所以需要轉發，是因為驗證請求用的金鑰無法安全地放進 App 裡。
  • 保留多久：轉發服務會把文字與產生的音訊在記憶體中保留最多一小時，讓相同的請求不必重新生成。不會寫入任何資料庫或請求紀錄，也不會與你或你的裝置產生任何關聯。
  • 完成的鬧鈴音檔留在你的手機上。它存放在 App 自己的儲存空間，你更換它或刪除 App 時就會一併刪除。

Google 為了產生語音而處理這段文字，適用 Google 的隱私權政策。我們使用付費方案，Google 聲明該方案送出的內容不會用於訓練其模型。

如果你從不使用這個功能，你寫的任何內容都不會離開手機。

第三方服務

當你輸入地址或點選「排程智慧鬧鐘」時，App 會代表你使用下列服務：

  • iPhone — Apple Maps（MapKit / CLGeocoder）：將地址文字轉換為座標並計算通勤路線。
  • Android — Google Maps Platform：以 Maps SDK 與 Routes API 繪製路線並計算距離；地址轉換為座標則由 Android 內建的地理編碼器在裝置上完成。
  • 兩個平台 — Apple Weather / WeatherKit：取得住家、公司以及路線沿途各點的天氣預報。

Apple Maps 與 Apple Weather / WeatherKit 由 Apple Inc. 提供，並受 Apple 的 隱私權政策約束。Google Maps Platform 由 Google LLC 提供，並受 Google 的 隱私權政策約束。你的地址與座標只會用於計算路線與取得天氣預報。

Android 版的天氣請求

Apple 要求天氣請求必須以私鑰簽章，而該私鑰無法安全地放進 Android App，因此 Android 版是透過我們自行營運的一個小型轉發服務向 Apple 取得預報，而非直接連線。這對你的意義是：

  • 轉發服務只會收到一組座標、一段時間範圍與語言設定，不會收到你的地址文字、裝置識別碼，或任何能識別你身分的資訊。
  • 座標在使用前會被四捨五入到約一公里的精度，取得的預報會在記憶體中保留約十五分鐘，讓同一地區的重複查詢不必再次向 Apple 索取。
  • 不會寫入任何資料庫或請求紀錄，也不會提供給任何第三方。

iPhone 版沒有這個轉發服務，它直接與 Apple Weather 連線。

廣告

Rainy Clock 會透過 Unity LevelPlay 顯示一則橫幅廣告；LevelPlay 是由 Unity Technologies 營運的廣告中介平台（前身為 ironSource）。Unity 與透過它競價的廣告夥伴可能會處理裝置、廣告與互動資料，用於提供、限制、衡量與改善廣告。LevelPlay 受 Unity 的 隱私權政策約束。

追蹤

Android 版沒有「App 追蹤透明度」提示——這個機制在該平台上並不存在。廣告是否個人化，完全取決於本節末尾所述的同意選擇，由 App 傳遞給廣告 SDK。本節其餘內容描述的是 iPhone 版。

第一次開啟 App 時，iOS 會詢問是否允許 Rainy Clock 追蹤你在其他公司 App 與網站上的活動。這是 Apple 的「App 追蹤透明度」提示，你的選擇決定接下來的行為：

  • 選擇允許：裝置上的廣告識別碼可能會提供給 Unity 及透過 LevelPlay 競價的廣告夥伴，讓橫幅廣告更貼近你的興趣。
  • 選擇不允許，或從未被詢問：廣告會以非個人化方式請求，廣告識別碼不會用於追蹤你。

你可以隨時在「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 追蹤」更改這個選擇。無論選擇哪一種，App 的功能完全相同，差別只在廣告的關聯性。

若你位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士，在任何廣告載入前還會出現一個獨立的同意對話框，之後可隨時透過 App 內的「廣告隱私設定」重新調整。

鬧鐘與通知

在 iOS 26 以上，鬧鐘透過 Apple 的 AlarmKit 排程，這也是鬧鐘能穿透靜音與專注模式的原因；在 iOS 17 至 25 則以本機通知排程。兩種方式都完全在你的裝置上處理，鬧鐘與通知內容不會傳送到任何伺服器。

兒童隱私

本 App 不會刻意收集 13 歲以下兒童的資料。
